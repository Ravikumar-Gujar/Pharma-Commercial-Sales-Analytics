# Pharma Commercial & Sales Analytics

## Project Overview

An end-to-end pharmaceutical sales analytics project focused on identifying product performance, demand patterns, seasonality, portfolio concentration, and commercial opportunities using data analytics and business intelligence techniques.

The project analyzes pharmaceutical sales data across hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly granularities and converts the findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Pharmaceutical companies need to understand changing product demand and sales patterns to improve commercial planning, inventory decisions, and portfolio prioritization.

This project aims to answer:

- Which pharmaceutical products contribute the most to overall sales volume?
- How does product demand change over time?
- What seasonal patterns exist in pharmaceutical sales?
- Which products show high demand volatility?
- What are the strongest and weakest periods of sales?
- Where are potential commercial opportunities and risks?
- What recommendations can be derived from the analysis?

## Objectives

1. Analyze pharmaceutical sales performance across products.
2. Identify temporal and seasonal demand patterns.
3. Evaluate product contribution and portfolio concentration.
4. Measure demand volatility across products.
5. Develop business-oriented insights from sales data.
6. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
7. Translate analytical findings into actionable commercial recommendations.

## Dataset

The project uses four datasets containing pharmaceutical sales observations at different time granularities:

- Hourly sales data
- Daily sales data
- Weekly sales data
- Monthly sales data
